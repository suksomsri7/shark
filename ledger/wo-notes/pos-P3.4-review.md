# P3.4 S HUNTER (fraud + money) @ eca8f126: verdict = FINDINGS N1..N5 (1 HIGH · 2 MEDIUM · 2 LOW). Not CLEAN.
Method: read offline-sync.ts, the register/service/staff-pin/kds hunks, the route, ops/sync.ts and the migration, plus a pure probe (`p34-h/probe-h.mts`) that imports offline-shared.ts. No DB.

**N1 HIGH: manager accept gives the bill to a seller nobody verified, which raises the discount cap and priceOverride.** offline-sync.ts:128-136 (`tokenClaimUser` reads `u` without checking the signature) → stored at :511/:514 → used at :656 (`[p.sellerUserId, p.staffClaimUserId, p.syncedByUserId]`) → :390-393 (seller = that user if they can still sell).
Scenario: the cashier rings a bill with a discount above their own cap and sends it with `staffToken` = forged `base64url({"u":"<ownerId>"}).x`. The first pass rejects it STAFF_TOKEN_INVALID. When a manager clicks accept, submitRegisterSale runs with seller = OWNER, so the owner's unlimited cap and open-price lines apply, and the bill is recorded as sold by the owner. The cashier keeps the gap between the full price taken and the discounted bill. The same thing works with `sellerUserId=<ownerId>` and no token (rejected SELLER_MISMATCH first). The queue list makes it worse: :606 shows `sellerUserId ?? syncedByUserId`, not the token claim, so the manager sees the cashier.
Fix: keep only a verified seller in the payload (the token user when `staffActorFromToken` passes on the first pass, otherwise `syncedByUserId`). Never take authority from `p.sellerUserId` or `staffClaimUserId`. On accept, price with that verified user's caps (or the lower of that user's and the manager's). List claimed and verified seller as separate fields.

**N2 MEDIUM: cash can be moved out of a shift that is still open.** (a) :643-657 together with :368-369: accept always runs `shiftId=null`, whatever the reject reason. A cashier can force any reject (N1 forgery, bad seller), get a manager to accept, and the cash bill lands off-shift. Their open shift's expected cash then excludes it, so they can take that amount from the drawer. (b) :371-386: if the client leaves out `shiftId` and sets soldAt in a gap between two shifts of the same device (A closed 14:00, B open since 15:00, soldAt 14:30, snapshot issued before 14:30), no shift covers soldAt. When `requiredRegister` is off, the bill gets `shiftId=null` while B is open. Online, `resolveRegisterShift` would bind B.
Fix: on accept, re-resolve the device shift covering soldAt and bind it if it is OPEN; use null only when it is closed (SHIFT_CLOSED or SHIFT_REQUIRED). On the first pass, no shiftId plus no covering shift plus the device has an OPEN shift (or any shift in the last 24 h) should give SHIFT_REQUIRED and go to the manager queue, not off-shift.

**N3 MEDIUM: the client picks the pricing time (happy-hour backdating).** :363 bounds soldAt only by issuedAt and 24 h. :406 drops the snapshot-price path after `priceValidUntil`+15 min, but the price-at-soldAt path stays open for any soldAt in the window. The client gate refuses to sell past pvu+15 min (probe: `offlineSellGate` → PRICES_EXPIRED), so an honest client never sends such a soldAt.
Scenario: snapshot issued at 14:00 (pvu 15:00, happy hour 15:00-17:00). At 20:00 the cashier takes full price in cash and syncs `soldAt=16:00` with expected = happy-hour total. It is accepted OK with no drift and no audit flag. With a snapshot issued during happy hour and never refreshed, the window is the whole rule period inside 24 h.
Fix: CLOCK_INVALID when `evidence.priceValidUntil` is set and soldAt > pvu + OFFLINE_PRICE_GRACE_MS, to mirror the client gate. Also add `priceAtSyncSatang` to the audit and send to the manager queue any bill where price-at-soldAt < price-at-sync because of a time-window rule and syncedAt − soldAt is above a threshold (for example 30 min).

**N4 LOW-MEDIUM: an honest bill can be rejected (DoS) when its snapshot is pruned.** :96 (SNAPSHOT_KEEP=2) and :279-285. A device that sold on snapshot S1 and then fetched S2 and S3 before syncing (R2 refreshes every 15 min while online, on every pvu edge, and on reconnect flaps; sync is in BUSY backoff meanwhile) loses S1. Its bills then go SNAPSHOT_UNKNOWN → manager queue, cash in the drawer with no bill. This is not a forgery path: a forged or other-device hash still gives SNAPSHOT_UNKNOWN (:322 filters on deviceId).
Fix: keep every snapshot issued in the last 24 h (`OFFLINE_MAX_AGE_MS`, with a cap of about 200 per device), or prune only snapshots with no PROCESSING/REJECTED rows referencing them.

**N5 LOW: queue limbo states.** (a) :682-684 lets a manager discard a stale PROCESSING row (older than 5 min) whose PosSale already committed (crash between createSale and settle). The sale exists, the row says DISCARDED, and the device gets DISCARDED on resync, so the manager may book a false cash variance. Fix: before discarding, look up the PosSale by `reg2:<key>` and settle to SYNCED if it exists. (b) A crash after the claim at :638 leaves the row ACCEPTED_BY_MANAGER with saleId null. The list (:587, REJECTED only) hides it and discard refuses it (:682). Fix: include ACCEPTED rows with saleId null in the list.
