## 3. Contract
R1 **Files.** new `hr/availability.ts`, pure `hr/availability-rules.ts` · `hr/index.ts`: the SVC B.1 stub block becomes `export { staffWindows } from "./availability"` + type re-exports (field lists byte-identical, marker `HR H4.1`) · `outbox-consumers.ts` one marked hunk (the `hr.leave.decided` handler) · `ui.tsx` and/or `hr/leave/page.tsx` (impact line) · `src/messages/{th,en}/common.json` `hr.leaveImpact.*` · `hr-ui-inventory.json`. **Frozen** (S1.4): `rules.ts` (C-2) · `roster.ts` · `roster-rules.ts` · `swap*.ts` · `time-rules.ts` · `leave.ts` · `leave-rules.ts` · `service.ts` · `payroll*.ts` · `privacy-shared.ts` · `pos-seam.ts` · `booking/**` · `approval/**` · `approval-effects.ts` · `prisma/**` · seed · regression oracles.
R2 **Pure `dayWindows({day, unitId, entries, leaves})`** → `[{startMin,endMin}]` sorted, overlapping/touching merged, clipped to [0,1440]; no DB/clock; bad day ⇒ `[]`. `entries` = the employee's published entries dated D−1 and D (any unit), `leaves` = their leaves.
R3 **Window of day D** = entries dated D with unit = `unitId` or null → `[start, cross ? 1440 : end]`, plus entries dated D−1 with `crossesMidnight` → `[0, end]`. A leave applies to entries **by start day** (a shift belongs to the day it starts — H2.1 R3/H2.7 R4). Only `APPROVED` counts; PENDING/REJECTED/CANCELLED never change a window. Full-day or hours leave (Q5) on the start day removes the whole entry.
R4 **Half-day** (AM/PM): the day's span = earliest start → latest end (overnight end + 1440) over **all** the employee's entries dated that day (every unit); mid = start + floor(span/2); AM removes [start, mid), PM removes [mid, end). Vectors: 540–1080 ⇒ AM 810–1080 · PM 540–810 · split 540–720 + 1020–1260 ⇒ AM 1020–1260 · PM 540–720 · night 1320→360 ⇒ AM leaves D+1 120–360 · PM leaves D 1320–1440 + D+1 0–120.
R5 **Overnight** 22:00→06:00 started D: D ⇒ [1320,1440] · D+1 ⇒ [0,360]; leave on D+1 only keeps that tail.
R6 **Rows** (`staffWindows`): for each distinct input id in input order that is an employee of this tenant in an **active** HR system: "roster-managed for D" = ≥1 published entry dated in the ISO week (Mon–Sun, Bangkok) of D, or an overnight entry dated D−1 ⇒ row `{employeeId, windows, breaks: []}` (keys exactly these); otherwise **no row** (booking falls back as today). Roster-managed with nothing that day ⇒ `windows: []` (a rostered day off is not bookable).
R7 ENDED, `active=false`, or D outside `startDate…endDate` ⇒ row with `windows: []` (never a fallback).
R8 **Scope:** other-tenant ids, unknown ids, inactive HR system, bad date, empty ids ⇒ nothing (empty ids ⇒ no DB call). Unit: only entries of `unitId` or unit null open time; an entry in another unit that day leaves `[]` here (borrowed elsewhere). Published = `publishedAt ≠ null` (same as rosterOnDay, Q3). Reads only; no names, no leave type/reason.
R9 **Hint** `leaveDutyHints(ctx, viewer, {leaveId})` → `{ok:true, items}` | `{ok:false, reason: hintNo}`. Leave PENDING or APPROVED ⇒ items = the employee's published entries dated within the leave, ordered date → startMin → id; REJECTED/CANCELLED ⇒ `items: []`. Item keys exactly `entryId date unitId startMin endMin crossesMidnight part (FULL|AM|PM|HOURS) others candidates`. `others` = other employees with a published entry on the same date + shiftId + unit, active, not ENDED, not on an approved full-day leave that date. `candidates` = employees of the same system whose unit-of-day (H2.1 R3, borrowing counts) = entry unit, active, not ENDED, in employment, not the leaver, no approved leave covering that date, no published entry overlapping the entry's interval (overnight tails included); keys exactly `employeeId name weekMin weekMinAfter`; `weekMin` = paid minutes (end − start − break) of their published entries in that ISO week, skipping days with their approved full-day leave; `weekMinAfter` = weekMin + paid minutes of the entry; order weekMin ↑ → name (code-unit) → id. No limit on count (Q7).
R10 **Notify** `leaveDutyNotify({tenantId, systemId, leaveId})`, called by the `hr.leave.decided` consumer for status APPROVED only: one tx under `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('hr-leave-hint:<leaveId>',0))`; leave not APPROVED, or an AuditLog `hr.leave.duty_hint` targetId=leaveId exists ⇒ no-op; uncovered = items with `others = 0`; none ⇒ no-op (no audit); else `appNotification.createMany` to every member who passes the hint viewer rule (R11) except the leaver's linked user + one audit `{leaveId, uncovered, recipients}`. Title `มีกะที่ต้องหาคนแทน` · body `ใบลาที่อนุมัติแล้วทำให้กะ {n} กะยังไม่มีคนแทน — เปิดหน้าการลาเพื่อดูคนที่ว่าง` (no names, type or reason). Replays, concurrent consumers and the later REJECTED event write nothing more; a revoke does not retract (Q10). No new event/label.
R11 **Viewers of the hint** (same bytes `hintNo` for everyone else, unknown id, other tenant): OWNER · MANAGER with unitAccess `[]` or containing the employee's home unit · STAFF with `hr.leave.decide` under the same unit rule. Not: payroll key alone, requester key, plain member, the employee themself, other-branch manager.
R12 **UI (screen 05):** on each PENDING/APPROVED leave card of `/hr/leave` for hint viewers, one line `data-testid="hr-leave-impact"`: per item "กระทบ: <กะ> <วัน> (<เวลา>) — ยังมี {others} คน · ไม่ต้องหาคนแทน" or "— ขาด 1 คน → ระบบเสนอ <first candidate> (ชั่วโมงสัปดาห์ {weekMin}→{weekMinAfter})" with `hr-leave-impact-candidate-*` · no candidate ⇒ `noCandidate`.
