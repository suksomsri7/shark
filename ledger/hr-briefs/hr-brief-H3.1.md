R3 **One work day = one employee-day** (`buildWorkDays`, pure, integers, floor). Input `{employeeId, startDate, endDate, from, to, days:[{day, unitId, holiday:{id}|null, slots[], leaves[]}], rules?}`; slot `{dayId, version, slotKey, stored, final, stale, orphan, status, startMin, endMin, crossesMidnight, plannedBreakMin, workedMin, breakMin, lateMin, earlyOutMin, otMin, missingOut, leaveId, leaveFraction, rosterEntryId}`; leave `{id, status APPROVED|PENDING, halfDay, hours, paid, legacy}`. Output one row per day of [from,to] ∩ employment, keys `employeeId day periodKey unitId dayClass dayStatus` + the 13 minute keys + `leaveDays review sources entries digest`. `periodKey` = owner day's month (overnight shift belongs to its start day ⇒ its period). `rules` = `WORK_RULES_DEFAULT {normalDayMin 480, breakRule "PLANNED_AFTER_5H", breakAfterMin 300}`.
  - **Use only stored slots** (`stored && final`) for minutes; a live slot not final ⇒ `NOT_FINAL`, final but unstored ⇒ `NOT_STORED`.
  - **Class:** holiday ⇒ HOLIDAY · any shift slot (startMin ≠ null) or NO_SCHEDULE slot ⇒ WORKDAY · any DAY_OFF slot ⇒ DAY_OFF · no slot ⇒ UNSCHEDULED.
  - **Planned** per shift slot = window (cm ⇒ end+1440−start) − `plannedBreakMin` (roster entry `breakMin`; template 0), ≥ 0; plannedMin = Σ.
  - **Break rule (Q3, closes "H3 must" 1):** deduction d = workedMin > breakAfterMin ? min(max(0, plannedBreak − breakMin), workedMin − breakAfterMin) : 0; net = worked − d. Why: LPA s.27 rest after ≤ 5 h; unclocked planned break presumed taken, never below 5 h, never twice; continuous. (`CLOCKED_ONLY` ⇒ 0).
  - **Leave:** f = Σ `leaveFraction` of stored slots whose leaveId is an APPROVED non-hourly leave (cap 1.0); UNSCHEDULED day: f from leaves (half 0.5 / full 1.0). basis = plannedMin > 0 ? plannedMin : normalDayMin; per leave floor(frac × basis) → paid/unpaid by policy (`legacy` = no policy ⇒ paid + `LEGACY_LEAVE`). DAY_OFF/HOLIDAY days without shift: no leave minutes (H2.7 R4).
  - **Regular** pool = Σ over shift slots with status ≠ LEAVE of max(0, net − otMin); reg = min(pool, max(0, plannedMin − leaveMin)); unplannedMin = pool − reg. Worked minutes of LEAVE slots count nowhere (`WORKED_ON_LEAVE`).
  - **Absent** = max(0, min(Σ planned of ABSENT slots, plannedMin − leaveMin − Σ planned of attended slots)) (attended = ON_TIME/LATE/EARLY_OUT).
  - **Hourly leave (closes "H3 must" 4):** L = min(h×60, max(0, basis − leaveMin − reg)); offsets L against late, then early, then absent; paid/unpaid L; h×60 > L ⇒ `HOURLY_LEAVE_UNUSED`. leaveDays = f + Σ floor(h/8, 0.1) (cap 1.0, "N.N").
  - **OT (measured only, closes "H3 must" 5):** WORKDAY shift ⇒ otWorkdayMin = Σ otMin; HOLIDAY shift ⇒ reg → holidayWorkMin, ot → otHolidayMin; no-shift slot on DAY_OFF/HOLIDAY ⇒ holidayWorkMin += min(net, normalDayMin), otHolidayMin += max(0, min(otMin, net) − normalDayMin) (H2.9 R3); NO_SCHEDULE on WORKDAY ⇒ workMin += net. HOLIDAY day ⇒ holidayPaidMin = normalDayMin, late/early/absent 0. Approved-OT pay stays H2.9's `HrPayAdjustment` (no money here).
  - **dayStatus:** NOT_FINAL/NOT_STORED ⇒ PENDING › attended or worked minutes ⇒ WORKED › HOLIDAY › leaveDays ≥ 1.0 ⇒ LEAVE › absent ⇒ ABSENT › DAY_OFF/UNSCHEDULED › WORKED.
  - **review** (sorted, unique). Blocking `WE_BLOCKING`: `LEAVE_MISMATCH` (approved non-hourly leaves of a shift day ≠ f, or a stored leaveId not approved — F3) · `MISSING_OUT` · `NOT_FINAL` · `NOT_STORED` · `ORPHAN` · `PENDING_LEAVE` · `STALE` · `STRAY_IN` (shift slot + `DAY` slot on one day — minutes of the DAY slot excluded) · `WORKED_ON_LEAVE`. Info: `HOURLY_LEAVE_UNUSED` · `LEGACY_LEAVE`.
  - **sources** `{attendanceDays:[{id, version, slotKey}], rosterEntryIds, leaveIds, holidayId}` sorted; **entries** `[{kind, minutes}]` mirror the header (WORK = workMin …) in H1.3 enum order, zeros omitted; **digest** = sha256 of canonical output minus digest.
