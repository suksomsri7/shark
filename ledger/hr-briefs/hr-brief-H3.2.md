## 2. Decision — "OT pay by day class (1.5/2/3)"
Nothing left for H3.2. Requested OT (every multiplier) = H2.9 only. Holiday pay for daily/hourly staff (s.56, `holidayPaidMin`) and rostered holiday/day-off work without an OT request (s.62, `holidayWorkMin` not covered by `OT_REQUEST`) are **priced by H3.4** run v2 from `HrWorkDay` (§7 "H3.4 must"). **By construction H3.2 creates only `DEDUCTION` rows and, for its own over-deduction, `ALLOWANCE` refunds — never kind OT, never reads `otWorkdayMin otHolidayMin holidayWorkMin holidayPaidMin` nor `OT_REQUEST` rows** (static check) ⇒ no double payment with H2.9.
