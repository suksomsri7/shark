## 3. Contract

- `claimClassOf({kind, sourceType, sourceRef}) → "ADVANCE"|"DAMAGES"|"OVERPAY"|null` — hoist H3.4's `clsOf` here and use it in `planCarryRows` too (one rule).
- `accrualAmountsV2(items: {employeeId, grossSatang, ssoEmployeeSatang, ssoEmployerSatang, whtSatang, netSatang, snapshotJson}[], bound: {id, employeeId, kind, sourceType, sourceRef, amountSatang}[]) → {ok:true, amounts: PayrollAccrualAmounts} | {ok:false, code:"INCONSISTENT", employeeId|null}`.
