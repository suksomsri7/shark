## 0. Premise
H2.3 = **service + approval wiring + one effect + minimal request surfaces**. A staff member asks to hand one of their published future shifts to a colleague (give-away) or to trade it for one of the colleague's (swap); the colleague accepts or declines; the request then goes to the approval core like a leave (chain if the shop set one, otherwise a direct HR decision); on approval both roster rows are rewritten in one transaction. No new table, no new page, no board card (H2.2 D2), no self-service create form (H2.10 "ขอเปลี่ยนกะ"), no impact/money preview (H2.11). Nothing that exists changes behaviour: H2.1 engine paths, `clock()`, kiosk, `monthlyAttendance`, MONTHLY payroll, leave chain, privacy matrix — all digests equal.
