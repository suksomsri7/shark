## Controller rulings (binding)
1. **Intent per pay row**: when the cashier picks PROMPTPAY (or CARD with Beam enabled) for the remaining amount, the dialog calls `createPaymentIntentAction` with `idempotencyKey = <cart idempotencyKey>:<method>:<amountSatang>` (CD3) and shows the right panel: QR from `qrPayload` (render with the existing QR component used by the receipt QR; EMV text for PromptPay, Beam hosted URL for `CARD_BEAM` as QR + "เปิดลิงก์"), amount, countdown to `expiresAt`, status line: `PROMPTPAY_STATIC` ⇒ "ให้ลูกค้าสแกน แล้วกดยืนยันเมื่อเห็นเงินเข้า" + button "ยืนยันเองเมื่อเห็นเงินเข้า"; `PROMPTPAY_BEAM` ⇒ radio-style line "รอเงินเข้า… ยืนยันอัตโนมัติผ่าน Beam" + the manual button (disabled with hint when `manualConfirmRequiresManager` and the user lacks `pos.shift.manage` — read the permission from the page props, do not guess); `CARD_BEAM` ⇒ "รอผลจาก Beam…" and **no** manual button. Poll `paymentIntentStatusAction` every 2 s while PENDING; stop on PAID/EXPIRED/CANCELLED.
2. **PAID** ⇒ the pay row is marked paid (✓ chip "เงินเข้าแล้ว · via Beam/ยืนยันเอง"), `reference = intent.id` is set on that row, the row amount is locked; the "ยืนยันรับเงิน" button becomes enabled only when every non-cash row is PAID (cash rows unchanged). Changing the amount/method of a row that has a PENDING intent ⇒ `cancelPaymentIntentAction` then a new intent; a PAID intent cannot be edited (toast "เงินเข้าแล้ว ใช้กับบิลนี้ หรือคืนเงินเอง") — only removing the row after cancelling is impossible: show the intent as locked.
3. **EXPIRED** ⇒ panel shows "QR หมดอายุ" + "สร้าง QR ใหม่" (new key suffix `:r<n>`); **CANCELLED** ⇒ row returns to unpaid. Refusals mapped through `intentRefusalMessageKey` and `refusalMessageKey`; `PROMPTPAY_NOT_CONFIGURED` ⇒ panel says "ร้านยังไม่ได้ตั้งพร้อมเพย์" with a link to accounting business profile; `CARD_UNAVAILABLE` or `beam.enabled=false` ⇒ the card tile subtitle "EDC · ใส่เลขอ้างอิง" and the old P1.6 reference flow (no intent).
4. **Submit** unchanged except the `reference` rows; `INTENT_*`/`AMOUNT_MISMATCH` refusals shown on the dialog; `PayDone` shows the method line with "via Beam" when the payment note says so (read from the sale result if present, else omit).
