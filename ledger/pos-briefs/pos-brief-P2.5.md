## 2. Requirements (S = server; U in §9)
- **R1 Plan model (S, CD2).** One **OPEN** split plan per session (partial unique). Plan = `{mode ITEM|EQUAL|SEAT, partCount 2..20, itemsHash, billDiscount?, serviceChargeBp, base/discount/service/total satang, version}` + parts `{no 1..N, label?, memberId?, base/discount/service/total, status OPEN|PAID, saleId?}` + lines `{partId, itemId, qty? | shareNum/shareDen, amountSatang, stockOwner}`. Covers **exactly** the session's unpaid items at creation (`expectedItemsHash` = `tableItemsHash` of the quote, else `TABLE_ITEMS_CHANGED`); every unit of every item allocated (ITEM: per-unit to parts; SEAT: per-unit to seats or shared; EQUAL: all shared by all) — unallocated ⇒ `VALIDATION` "ยังมีรายการที่ยังไม่ได้เลือกบิล/ที่นั่ง" (15C footnote); empty part ⇒ `VALIDATION`.
- **R2 Allocation (pure `table-split-shared.ts`, client-safe, deterministic, integer only, CD4).** (1) Item gross `g = qty × (unitPrice + optionsTotal)`. Whole units: part gets `k × each`. Shared units: `g_shared = q·m + r` over the m sharing parts ⇒ `q` each, `+1` satang to the first `r` by part number. (2) Part base `b_i`. (3) Table bill discount `D = discountOf(billDiscount, Σb)` (same rule as `priceCart`) allocated ∝ `b_i` by **largest remainder, ties → lower part number** ⇒ `d_i`. (4) Service charge `S = roundHalfUp((Σb − D) × bp, 10000)` — **the single-bill value** — allocated ∝ `(b_i − d_i)` by largest remainder ⇒ `s_i`. (5) `t_i = b_i − d_i + s_i`; **Σ t_i = Σb − D + S = whole-table grand exactly**. (6) VAT per part = `splitIncludedVat(t_i)` at the part's own bill level (COMMON rule 1); `|Σ vat_i − vat(whole)| ≤ ⌊(N+1)/2⌋` satang (each sale books its own VAT; no cross-part correction). EQUAL with `T = qN + r` ⇒ parts 1..r get `q+1`. Server recomputes and stores; stored = pure result (oracle).
- **R3 Part sale lines (S, CD3).** Whole-unit allocation ⇒ normal table line (`productId`, options, `itemId`/`components` exactly as `regTableLines` + P2.4 F6 rules) with `qty = k`, `unitPriceSatang = each`. Shared allocation ⇒ **share line**: name `"<item> (1/4)"` (fraction label), `qty 1`, `unitPriceSatang = share satang`, `productId` + options kept, **no `itemId`**; the item's **stock owner** part (lowest part number holding a share) carries the item's full stock as `components` (`itemId` → `{invItemId, qty: item.qty}`; recipe components × item.qty), others carry none ⇒ InvItem/BOM cut exactly once. `billDiscountSatang = d_i`, `serviceChargeSatang = s_i` (not recomputed from bp), `sourceModule "POS"`, `sourceId = sessionId`, channel = table channel (P2.4 Q4). No new `createSale` field.

Member/coupon/voucher/points: only on the part whose `memberId` is set (session member defaults to part 1; editable in the plan; one member per part); applied by normal register math on top of `t_i` (CD5). Tip, intents (P1.7), full tax invoice (P1.13, 15A) per part as today. ## 4. Accounting / stock
- **Each part = a normal register sale**: `pos.sale.paid` → account bridge JV per part (as P2.4 P7: Dr pay channel / Cr 4000 incl. service charge / Cr 2200 output VAT), receipt/ABB number per part (N parts = N consecutive seq), member points only on the member part, shift = paying device's shift, channel STORE/QR_TABLE (no commission). Σ(revenue + VAT) over parts = Σ part grands = plan total − member/coupon discounts.
- **Must not double-post:** the plan, plan edits/cancel and the pre-bill post nothing (no JV, no receipt seq, no outbox); part replay = same sale; closing the session on the last part posts nothing; void of one part reverses only that part's JV/points/stock; refund of a part = normal CN, plan untouched.
- **Stock cut once:** whole-unit lines cut `k` each (Σ = item qty); shared items cut once on the stock-owner part (R3 components), 0 elsewhere; recipe (P2.3) components follow the same rule; void of the owner part restores the whole item once, re-pay cuts once. **MenuItem counters untouched** by any bill (consumed at send by `createOrderInTx`; register path never calls `consumeMenuStock`); no kitchen round for part sales (no `ticketForSale`; heal skips `sourceId`); P2.6 `stockTaken` irrelevant to table rounds.
- **Known effects (owner lines):** share lines make per-product **qty** in reports count shares (revenue correct) → P2.12; a partial refund of a share line with "restock" restocks the whole item (owner part); VAT of parts may differ from a single bill by ≤ ⌊(N+1)/2⌋ satang.
