## 2. Rules (server, oracle-tested)

A MANAGER/granted STAFF must hold it on **every unit linked to the POS** for system-wide sections (reuse `canManageAllLinkedUnits`, `receipt-settings.ts:56`); per-unit sections need it at that unit. `pos.settings.payment` (phantom) is either registered in the catalogue or replaced by `pos.settings.manage` (Q2). Money-changing sections (service charge rate, tip) stay **OWNER-only** as today.
- **R2 Settings overview (read)** `posSettingsOverview(ctx, actor, {systemId, unitId})` → `{ok:true, canEdit:{general, staff, caps, unitStock, payment}, general, caps, unitStock, serviceCharge, tip}` — every value comes from the existing pure parser of that key (no second parser); read allowed with `pos.sale.create` or `pos.settings.manage` at the unit (cashier = read-only view, same as 17A today). Other tenant / other system / unit not linked ⇒ `NOT_FOUND`.
- **R3 General writer** `updatePosGeneralSettings(ctx, actor, patch)` with exact keys `{heldCartExpireDays?, autoLockMinutes?, shift?: {requiredRegister?, requiredOtherSources?, blindClose?, overShortReasonSatang?, forceCloseAfterHours?}, weighedBarcode?: {enabled, rules[]}, receiptLocale?: "th"|"en"}` (+ `dayCutoffMinutes` only if Q9 takes it).
