1. 2. Multi-item callers (POS sale lines, bundles, accounting goods issue/receipt, PO receive, bulk count, transfer of several items) must not deadlock: lock items in a deterministic order (sorted item ids) or handle 40P01/serialization failure with ONE bounded retry of the whole transaction — choose per caller, justify, and prove with a test that two transactions touching items in opposite order both complete correctly.
